## 5. Intake
Create a short form asking:
- Student name
- Parent/contact name (if applicable)
- Email/phone
- Grade or college level
- Course/subject
- Current topic
- Goal (homework/test/full-course support)
- Upcoming test date
- Upload/link to worksheet or review sheet

## 6. Policies
Launch draft:
- 60-minute sessions
- Payment before session
- 24-hour cancellation/reschedule window
- Late cancellation/no-show may be charged in full
- No guarantee of a particular grade/test score
- Academic integrity: tutoring/support only; no impersonation or prohibited completion of graded work
