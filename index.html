FAQ

Common questions

Do you tutor online?

Yes. Sessions are held by Google Meet or Zoom, with a shared digital whiteboard or screen as needed.

Can I book just one session?

Yes. The launch model is pay-as-you-go. Recurring weekly sessions can be arranged if they are useful.

What should I send before the session?

Course name, current topic, upcoming test or assignment, and any worksheet or review sheet you want to focus on.

How do Google Meet / Zoom sessions work?

After you book, you’ll get a confirmation with the video link. Join a few minutes early with a quiet space, working device, and any materials ready.

What is the cancellation policy?

Sessions canceled with at least 24 hours’ notice can be rescheduled. Late cancellations or no-shows may be charged in full. Confirm details when you book.
